Dear Professors Angrist, Hull, Pathak, and Walters,

I have a question that might interest you: How would you analyze publicly available data in Massachusetts to compare schools?

I really enjoyed your article *Race and the Mismeasure of School Quality*. I am building a free, open-source website (https://locovote.com/) to make it easy to compare schools and towns. My current plan is to let users choose what metric they want to use to compare schools:

1. Race-balanced progress
2. Test score progress
3. Test score levels

Personally, I'm inclined to focus most on race-balanced progress, but I'm a little nervous about extrapolating your results from urban districts and applying them to suburbs and rural areas. I would be curious to hear your thoughts.
